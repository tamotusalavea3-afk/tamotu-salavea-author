About the Book

The marks were never meant to be permanent.

Toa thought he understood the power of the tatau. He was wrong.

When ancient forces begin disturbing the balance between the living and the dead, Toa and those closest to him are drawn toward a darkness unlike anything they have faced before. In the shadowed realm of Pulotu, old truths begin to surface—and the meaning behind the sacred marks of Samoa takes on a terrifying new significance.

Something is changing. The boundaries between worlds are weakening. The spirits are restless. And an ancient power has begun questioning whether humanity deserves the gifts it was given.

As Toa, Tua, Fetu, and their allies confront forces beyond their understanding, they are forced to face more than an enemy. They must confront their own choices, their failures, and the consequences of what they have become.

Because when the marks that connect people to something greater begin to disappear, one question remains: What happens when creation itself decides who is worthy to remain?
