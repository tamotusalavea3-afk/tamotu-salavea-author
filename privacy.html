PRIVACY

Your information, handled simply.

Information submitted through reviews

If you submit a review, the website may store the name you provide, your rating, your review text, the book being reviewed, and limited technical information used to help prevent duplicate or abusive submissions. Review submissions are reviewed manually before publication.

How information is used

Submitted information is used to operate the reader-review feature, prevent spam or duplicate submissions, and display approved reviews publicly. Your review should not contain private or sensitive information you do not want displayed.

Contact

If you contact the author by email, your message and email address are used only to respond to your inquiry and manage legitimate correspondence.

Third-party services

Links to external retailers, including Lulu, are governed by those services' own privacy practices when you leave this website.

Questions

For questions about this website or privacy practices, contact contact@tamotusalavea.com.
