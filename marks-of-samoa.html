About the Book

When Toa returns to Samoa in search of identity, tradition, and the meaning behind the sacred marks of his ancestors, he expects a journey of culture and self-discovery. Instead, he is pulled into a hidden world where ancient spirits, forgotten powers, and family secrets awaken around him. As old legends step out of the shadows, Toa must confront the truth of who he is, where he comes from, and what his marks truly mean. With danger rising across the islands and a darkness seeking to reclaim its power, he discovers that heritage is more than blood—it is courage, sacrifice, and the strength to stand when the past calls your name. Marks of Samoa is a thrilling new take on traditional lore, blending Samoan culture, mythology, and adventure into a story of identity, legacy, and the power carried in every mark.
